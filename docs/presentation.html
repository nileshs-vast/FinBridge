Problem Statement

The Invoice Pile Problem

Indian SMEs manually key hundreds of vendor bills every month — at a cost their accountants pay every day

Cost of Manual Entry

  • 8–12 min per invoice × hundreds of bills per month
  • 3–5% error rate — surfaces only at month-end reconciliation
  • Zero audit trail — no record of who entered what, or when
  • Accountants doing data entry instead of advisory work
This affects every SME working with an external accounting firm. FinBridge is the fix.

What Breaks When This Fails

  • GST filings with wrong input-tax-credit amounts
  • Payments sent to the wrong vendor account
  • Salary disbursements booked under the wrong cost centre
  • MIS reports delivered late — underlying data isn't clean

Solution

FinBridge

A SaaS workspace shared between accounting firms and their clients — upload to approval in under 60 seconds

① Upload

Company user uploads any document — vendor invoice (PDF or image), salary register, bank statement. Any format, any Indian vendor.

② Extract

Claude vision or Gemini extracts vendor name, GSTIN, invoice #, date, CGST/SGST/IGST, and line items in 2–5 seconds.

Per-field confidence scores tell accountants exactly which values to double-check.

③ Review & Approve

Accountant sees the original document and extracted fields side-by-side with color-coded confidence badges.

One click to approve. Full audit trail written automatically.

  • Multi-company: one accountant, all client companies in a single queue
  • Offline-capable: fixture provider — 10 real Indian invoice PDFs bundled
  • Three-tier tenancy: Platform → Firm → Company, service-layer isolation
  • Full audit trail: every change logged with user + timestamp, shown in UI

Technical Architecture

One Stack, Three Tiers, Three AI Providers

Single public port · Application-layer tenant isolation · Pluggable AI

Tenancy Model

Platform Admin
  └── Accounting Firm  (Sharma & Co.)
        ├── Accountants  (all companies)
        ├── Company A:  Acme Manufacturing
        │     └── Company Users  (upload)
        └── Company B:  Lumen IT
              └── Company Users  (upload)

Every DB row scoped by firm_id + company_id. Single tenant_scope() FastAPI dependency enforces isolation — verified by a cross-tenant smoke test on every build.

Deployment

nginx :80  (public)
  └── proxy_pass → app :8000  (internal)
        ├── /api/*   FastAPI routes
        └── /*       React 18 SPA
postgres :5432  (internal only)
